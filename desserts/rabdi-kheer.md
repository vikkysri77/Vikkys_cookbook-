# Rabdi Kheer

A creamy and luxurious Indian rice pudding infused with saffron and caramel. This classic dessert is smooth, rich, and perfect for special occasions.

## Prep Time
35 minutes (including soaking)

## Cook Time
25 minutes

## Servings
6

## Ingredients

### For the Kheer
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 1 liter whole milk
- 1/2 cup condensed milk
- 1/4 tsp saffron strands
- 1/4 cup white sugar (for caramel)
- 2 tbsp ghee or butter
- 1/4 tsp cardamom powder (optional)
- Chopped pistachios for garnish
- Chopped almonds for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Rice**
   - Soak basmati rice in water for 30 minutes
   - Drain the water completely
   - Grind the soaked rice to a coarse, powder-like consistency using a grinder or food processor

2. **Infuse the Saffron**
   - Soak saffron strands in 1/4 cup warm milk for 10 minutes to release flavor and color

3. **Start the Milk Base**
   - Bring 1 liter of whole milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pot
   - Stir occasionally to prevent sticking

4. **Cook the Rice**
   - Add the ground rice to the boiling milk, stirring constantly to avoid lumps
   - Mix well and cook on medium heat for 10-12 minutes, stirring frequently
   - The rice should become soft and creamy

5. **Add Saffron**
   - Pour the saffron-infused milk into the rice mixture
   - Stir well and cook for another 2-3 minutes

6. **Prepare the Caramel**
   - In a separate pan, melt the white sugar over medium heat, stirring occasionally
   - Continue cooking until it turns golden brown and becomes watery (caramel stage)
   - Remove from heat immediately to prevent burning

7. **Combine with Condensed Milk**
   - Add condensed milk to the rice mixture
   - Stir well to incorporate

8. **Add the Caramel**
   - Carefully pour the hot caramel into the kheer mixture
   - Stir thoroughly to distribute the caramel throughout
   - Add cardamom powder if desired
   - Cook for 1-2 more minutes

9. **Finish**
   - Remove from heat and let cool slightly
   - Stir in ghee or butter
   - Garnish with chopped pistachios and almonds

## Notes

- **Consistency**: Rabdi Kheer should be creamy and pourable, not too thick. The rice will continue to absorb milk as it cools, so don't overcook
- **Saffron**: For enhanced flavor and aroma, use good quality saffron strands
- **Caramel Safety**: Be careful when handling hot caramel - it's extremely hot and can cause burns
- **Make Ahead**: This dessert tastes even better when served chilled the next day
- **Storage**: Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 3 days
- **Variations**: 
  - Add a pinch of rose water for floral notes
  - Use condensed milk mixed with evaporated milk for less sweetness
  - Top with additional nuts and dried fruits
- **Serving**: Serve warm or chilled, traditionally served during festivals and special celebrations
